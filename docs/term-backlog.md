# Term backlog

Everyday sailing vocabulary that is not in the game yet, collected for future
lessons. The Estonian definitions are short summaries of the
[Mereviki](http://mereviki.vta.ee) articles (the former Veeteede Amet wiki,
read from the Wayback Machine) unless another source is named. English
equivalents marked "?" still need checking.

Sources: **MV** = Mereviki article, **PUR** = EKI Purjetamise terminibaas
(sonaveeb.ee/ds/pur).

## Review of the current terms against Mereviki

- 35 of the game's Estonian main names are Mereviki headwords as such (vöör,
  ahter, tekk, kiil, rool, mast, poom, grootpuri, foka, kokpit, kajut,
  ahtripeegel, veeliin, süvis, pakpoord, tüürpoord, ankur, tihttuul, pautimine,
  halssimine, ankrukett, ankrukäpp, ankrupeli, ankrupall, merepõhi, kai, pollar,
  tuletorn, lainemurdja, poi, plokk, seekel, päästevest, päästeparv,
  signaaltuli); 10 more match through an accepted name (saaling, reeling,
  vall, kinnitusotsad, brass, topp, parduun, F, Q, raadio). No conflicts found.
- Added: **rumpel** and **roolipuu** are now accepted for roolipinn (MV: Rumpel).
- Note: in Mereviki **ruhv** is the crew's shared living space, not the cabin
  roof. The game only accepts it silently for "kajut"; consider dropping it.

## Ideas for exercises without a picture

Groups E and F are words rather than things. They could use:
- description → term and term → meaning (already in the game)
- a sentence with a gap: "Enne tormi tuli purjed ___ ." (rehvida)
- matching pairs: five Estonian words to five English ones
